# Meridian 1.2.0

Move your desktop clocks into place and find them easily from the menu bar. Choose regions from a worldwide directory you can browse and search offline.

- Drag the **Meridian title bar** to move the desktop panel. Release near a screen edge to snap into place.
- Open the menu bar panel and choose **Open** to bring the widget forward. Close the desktop panel with **×** to send it back to the menu bar without quitting.
- Use **More → Find panel (reset position)** to recover the panel. Snapping and position locking are optional.
- Browse **400+ supported time zones and representative cities**, grouped by geographical region.
- Search English or Simplified Chinese city names, countries, common country aliases and time-zone identifiers.
- See each region's country and current UTC offset before adding it.
- Start with popular places, or filter Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, Oceania, islands and Antarctica.
- Search worldwide when a regional filter has no match.
- Get weather using representative location coordinates, including places outside the original favorites.
- Recognize legacy time-zone names without offering duplicate spellings; keep distinct representative cities that share clock rules.

The default remains about four clocks, with up to six selected clocks. Desktop and menu bar choices remain independent. The selectable directory is worldwide; it is not limited to the favorite list. Available entries follow the time zones supported by your macOS version.

Download **Meridian-1.2.0-macOS.dmg**, open it and drag Meridian.app onto Applications. The ZIP is used for signed Sparkle updates. Universal for Apple Silicon and Intel; macOS 14 or later.

This build is ad-hoc signed and is **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may block first launch under its security policy.

## 简体中文

现在可拖动顶部 Meridian 标题栏移动面板，松手后吸附屏幕边缘。点击状态栏图标 → **打开** 即可打开或唤起；桌面面板的 **×** 只收起到状态栏，程序继续运行。找不到面板时，可在 **更多 → 找回面板（重置位置）** 恢复位置。

新增全球地区目录：可浏览 **400 多个时区及代表城市**，按地理区域筛选，支持中英文城市名、国家和时区名称搜索。选择前可查看国家与当前 UTC 偏移；地区名称和搜索离线可用，天气使用代表地点坐标查询。

默认约 4 个时钟，最多同时添加 6 个，桌面和状态栏分别选择。可选地区覆盖全球，不再只显示常用列表。请下载 **DMG 安装包**；ZIP 保留用于签名自动更新。仅限非商业使用，此版尚未经过 Apple 公证。
