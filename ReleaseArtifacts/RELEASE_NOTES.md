# Meridian 1.1.0

World clocks, full dates and local weather on your Mac desktop and menu bar.

- Universal application for Apple Silicon and Intel; requires macOS 14 or later.
- Full year, month, day and weekday for every clock, including previous/next-day labels.
- Choose one or more desktop and menu bar clocks independently; up to six regions.
- Local weather from Open-Meteo, with unavailable and outdated states.
- New orbital-clock app icon, also shown in the menu bar.
- Fixed dropdown clipping and offsets; all clocks remain accessible on smaller displays.
- English by default, with live Simplified Chinese switching.
- Sparkle automatic update checks, optional background downloads and Ed25519-signed archives.

## Download

Download **Meridian-1.1.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it and move **Meridian.app** to Applications. `SHA256SUMS` contains the archive checksum. `appcast.xml` is the signed update feed used by the app.

This first release uses ad-hoc Apple code signing and is **not notarized**. macOS may block first launch under its security policy. Sparkle's archive signature authenticates updates; it is separate from Apple notarization.
